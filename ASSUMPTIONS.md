# Assumptions

- All travellers in a room leave from the same starting city. Friday–Sunday is the default weekend. On Saturday or Sunday, “This weekend” uses the remaining days. Custom trips span one to seven inclusive calendar days in India Standard Time.
- The lowest submitted comfort budget governs the shortlist. The initial budget is a suggestion. Transport includes both last-mile legs. Overnight outbound travel can replace one paid night. Food and local travel use inclusive calendar days.
- The displayed match is a weighted planning score. An unavailable forecast contributes a neutral weather score. Route, cost, crowd and transfer estimates require operator and local verification. A late-night activity flag describes pickup availability.
- The host’s reveal opens the shortlist for all visitors. Anyone with the room link can vote or reveal after the first vote. Each traveller’s selected transport is carried into their own Parent Pack and share card.
- Production requires Upstash. Local development without credentials uses temporary process memory for room, quota and analytics testing. Anonymous room-specific participant identifiers are distinct from the browser analytics ID.
- Analytics counts browser IDs. Clearing storage and switching devices affect unique counts. CSV and dashboard use IST days. Family contact blanks are filled privately after downloading or copying a brief.
